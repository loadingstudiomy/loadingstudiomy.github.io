Way To Hell

Tilt to fall! Guide a tiny snail from outer space all the way down to Hell.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

In the game

No buttons — just tilt your phone. Land on the stairs, dodge the traps and see how deep you can go.

Fall through five worlds

Start among the stars, drop through the clouds, crash past the forest, dig through glowing crystal caves and keep going into Hell itself. Every world has its own big moments happening around you.

Watch your step

Crumbling stairs, icy steps, sideways wind, swinging platforms, lava, lightning clouds and thorns. The deeper you go, the faster it gets.

Eat chillies, go meteor

Fill the heat meter and your snail becomes a flaming meteor that smashes straight through everything in its way.

Come back stronger

Spend coins on permanent upgrades and shop items like starting dashes and a protective shell.

Fair & offline

Optional ads only — watch one to revive or double your coins, never forced. Your progress stays on your device.

How deep can you fall?

Abducted by a UFO, one tiny snail breaks out of the hull… and starts falling. All the way down.

  • Simple tilt controls, easy to pick up
  • Five hand-crafted voxel worlds from space to Hell
  • Chunky pixel-art UI and a very expressive snail
  • Permanent upgrades and a shop
